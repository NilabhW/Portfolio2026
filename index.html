How I Work

The Process

01
Discover & Research

I start with questions, not answers. Competitor audits, audience mapping, and cultural research to understand what already exists — and what's missing.

02
Concept & Ideate

Sketching fast, killing darlings faster. Concept exploration through rough thumbnails, mood boards, and reference deep-dives before committing to a direction.

03
Design & Refine

Building in high fidelity — typography locked, colour tested, spacing obsessed over. Iteration until the design is inevitable rather than arbitrary.

04
Present & Deliver

Final files, brand guidelines, mockups, and a handover that actually makes sense. The work continues after the file is closed.
