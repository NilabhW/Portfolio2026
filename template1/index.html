About

The Designer

Blending logic with creativity

I'm a Computer Science & Design student exploring the intersection of technology and visual communication. From bold poster layouts to refined brand systems, I believe great design is born where empathy meets precision.

Whether it's crafting an identity for a startup or designing packaging that tells a story, I pour intention into every pixel and every curve. My work is driven by a love for typography, color, and the small details that make a design feel alive.
